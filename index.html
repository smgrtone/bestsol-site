Practical, profitable, adoptable.

Carbon projects can only succeed when GHG-reducing practices are practical, profitable, and grounded in what farmers can truly adopt. We design our projects from the bottom up—driven by science but guided by what works for farmers. If it benefits them, we make it happen.

02 / Beyond Methane

Not just methane, but nitrous oxide.

While global efforts rightly target methane reduction in rice paddies, rising nitrous oxide emissions—especially under alternative drainage and high nitrogen use—can offset those gains. We have developed farmer-ready practices that cut both methane and nitrous oxide, unlocking greater carbon reduction potential from the latter.
